Nalee Kim achieved "13rd LG future medical scientist award" !!

The competition rate of this award was 20 to 1 and evaluated by number of papers, number of papers per year, highest IF paper, second highest IF paper during fellow. ( only in case of SCI and 1st author )

Nalee Kim published 18 papers during her fellow, including Hepatocellular carcinoma study which accepted at *Journal of Hepatology(IF : 17.425)*in 2020.

Congratulation Nalee !!!
