The aim of the OpenKBP Challenge is to advance fair and consistent comparisons of dose prediction methods for knowledge-based planning (KBP).

We won 2nd place in the validation phase and 3rd place testing phase.

We collaborated with Sanchit Hira, a graduate student of Johns Hopkins University, as 'team\_overfitting.'
